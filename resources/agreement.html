Why Dopamine Gate Exists

This application is not designed to punish you. It exists to interrupt automatic behavior.

Time

Time is limited. Once spent, it cannot be recovered.

Attention

Attention determines what we experience, learn, remember, and reinforce. What repeatedly receives our attention becomes easier for the mind to return to.

Mental well-being

Immediate pleasure is not always the same thing as long-term well-being. An activity can feel good while still becoming harmful when its use becomes compulsive.

Why the gate asks you to pause

The purpose of this gate is to create a small amount of friction between an impulse and an action.

The goal is not to make decisions for you. The goal is to give you enough space to make the decision deliberately.

Your responsibility

You remain responsible for how you use the application. The gate cannot control you. It can only create a pause.
